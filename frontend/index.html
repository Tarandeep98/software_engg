Consensus Engine Event Stream

1. Leader Election

Randomized election timeouts prevent split votes. A candidate requires votes from a majority of nodes (> N/2 = 2 of 3) to claim leadership for a new monotonically increasing Term.

2. Log Replication & Commit

The Leader receives client writes, appends them locally, and broadcasts AppendEntries RPCs. A log entry is committed once replicated across majority quorum (2 nodes).

3. Quorum & Fault Tolerance

With N=3 nodes, fault tolerance is $f = \lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor = 1$. The cluster tolerates 1 node failure. If 2 nodes fail, quorum is impossible and writes are rejected to protect consistency.
